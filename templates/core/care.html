General guidance for a considered wardrobe. Always follow the care label on your own garment.

Give garments room

Air garments between wears and store them in a clean, dry place. Let items dry completely before putting them away.

Care for the shape

Fold knitwear for storage. Choose suitable hangers for structured garments and avoid crowding them together.

Follow the label

Washing, drying and cleaning requirements vary with fabric and construction. Consult the garment's label or a professional cleaner when uncertain.
